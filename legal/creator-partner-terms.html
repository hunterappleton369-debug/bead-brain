The agreement between you and Child Phenoms when you join the Creator Partner Program, get your link, and earn a commission on the kits parents order through it.

Version 1.0 · Effective date: November 4, 2026

In plain words: you get a link. When a parent orders the free kit through your link, you earn $10 on your first kit, $20 on each of kits 2 to 10, and $30 on every kit after that. A kit clears 30 days after the order if it was not refunded. We pay by PayPal once a month. Be honest in what you say, make clear you earn a commission, and do not count your own orders. Either of us can end it any time by email.

1. Who this agreement is between

These terms are an agreement between you and Enamelogic LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company doing business as Child Phenoms ("Child Phenoms", "we", "us"). You accept them by ticking the box on the application. If you do not agree, do not apply.

2. Who can join

You must be 18 or older, live in the United States, post mainly in English to a mainly US audience, and own the accounts you list on your application. We review every application by hand and may decline anyone for any reason. Approval is personal to you and the accounts you listed.

3. You are an independent contractor

You are not our employee, agent or legal partner. You decide what to make, when to post and how to run your accounts. You may not sign, promise or take money on our behalf. You pay your own expenses, including the $6.95 shipping on any kit you order for yourself.

4. Your link

We give you one link, childphenoms.com/go/CODE, where CODE is your partner code (your first name plus two digits, for example SARAH27). The link and the code remain ours and are licensed to you for as long as this agreement runs. You may not sell, lend or share them for anyone else's use, and you may not make up or use any other code or link.

5. How orders are credited to you

A kit order is credited to you when the parent reaches our site through your link and orders within 30 days of that click. There is no code to type at checkout. If a parent clicks more than one partner's link, the most recent click within the 30 days wins. The link is remembered in the browser the parent clicked it in; an order placed from a different device or browser cannot be matched to you. Our records of clicks and orders control. You can see your orders in your partner hub, and we will explain any credit we decline if you ask.

6. What earns a commission, and how much

You earn a commission on each Qualifying Kit Order: an order for the Child Phenoms starter kit that (a) was credited to you under section 5, (b) was paid, (c) was not refunded, cancelled or charged back within 30 days of the order, and (d) was not placed by you, by anyone in your household, or by anyone sharing your address or payment method.

Your 1st kit$10
Kits 2 through 10$20 each

The rate for a kit is set by how many Qualifying Kit Orders you had before it, counted when the order is paid. A reversed order (section 8) does not count toward the tiers. No commission is paid on the order-form add-on, on any course, membership or certification, or on anything else we sell, unless we say so in writing.

7. The 30-day hold and payouts

Each commission is held for 30 days from the order date, the same window as our keep-the-kit promise to parents. Once a month, around the 15th, we pay by PayPal every commission whose hold has ended. There is no minimum. Give us an accurate PayPal email before your first payout; if a payment fails because the details are wrong, we hold the amount and retry when you correct them. PayPal's fees are ours. We pay by no other method unless we agree in writing.

8. Refunds and chargebacks

If a kit is refunded, cancelled or charged back within the hold, it never becomes a commission. If a kit we already paid you for is later refunded or charged back, we deduct that commission from your next payout; a negative balance carries forward until new commissions clear it. We show every reversal in your partner hub.

9. Staying active

Publish your first video with your link within 14 days of approval, and at least one post a week that mentions Child Phenoms and carries your link after that. After 30 days with no such post we may pause your link: it still opens the kit page, but orders placed while it is paused are not credited to you. To reactivate, post with your link and email us the post. Commissions earned before a pause are not affected.

10. Telling people you earn a commission

The law (the FTC's Endorsement Guides, 16 CFR Part 255) requires you to make clear, in any post that mentions Child Phenoms or carries your link, that you earn a commission. You are responsible for meeting that rule and each platform's rules for commission content, for example TikTok's commercial content toggle and Instagram's paid partnership label. A simple "#ad" at the start of your caption, or saying "I earn a commission if you order through my link," is enough for us.

11. What you may say

Say what is true and what is on your partner hub. You may describe the benefits abacus training is known for, show public footage of abacus competitors as what is possible, and tell your own real experience. You may not: promise a specific result by a specific date; invent a customer story or present anyone as a Child Phenoms student who is not one; make medical or diagnostic claims (for example about ADHD, dyslexia or autism); quote any price or value other than that the kit is free and shipping is $6.95; use false urgency or scarcity; disparage a named school, teacher or person; or promote another abacus or math program in a post that mentions Child Phenoms. Children may appear in your content; an adult presents the offer and the link. Third-party clips we point you to are not ours: show the source on screen, keep cuts short, and you are responsible for your use of them.

12. What you may not do

You may not: claim commission on orders by you or your household; pay or reward anyone to order; post your link on coupon, deal or discount sites, in browser extensions, or in other people's comment sections; run paid ads that use the Child Phenoms name or a variation as a keyword, display URL or ad copy; send unsolicited bulk messages; use bots, bought engagement or fake accounts; register domains, handles or accounts containing "Child Phenoms" or a confusingly similar name; or misrepresent your relationship with us. We may reverse any commission connected to prohibited conduct and may end this agreement under section 15.

You grant Child Phenoms a non-exclusive, royalty-free, worldwide license to repost, embed, excerpt and promote any content you publish that mentions Child Phenoms, on our social accounts, website, emails and ads, always with credit to your handle, for the life of this agreement and 12 months after for content already reposted. 14. Confidentiality

Anything we share with partners that is not public (unreleased offers, prices, launch dates, performance figures, other partners' information) stays confidential until we make it public. The commission rates and these terms are not confidential.

15. Ending the agreement

Either side may end this agreement at any time, for any reason, by email. Your link stops crediting at once. Commissions already earned and cleared are paid on the normal schedule. If we end it for fraud, self-referral, paid or rewarded orders, or repeated banned claims, you forfeit unpaid commissions and must remove content that uses our name or assets. Sections 8, 13, 14, 16, 18 and 19 survive the end of the agreement.

16. Taxes

You are responsible for all taxes on what we pay you. If your payments reach a level where tax law requires information from you or a form from us, we will ask you for it then (for example a Form W-9) and may hold payouts until we have it.

We may change these terms, the commission rates or the activity rule by emailing you and posting the new version at this address at least 14 days before it takes effect. Changes apply to orders placed after the effective date. If you do not agree, stop posting and tell us; we will end the agreement and pay what you have earned. Posting after the effective date means you accept the change.

18. Limits on liability

Our total liability under this agreement is limited to commissions earned and unpaid when the claim is made. You will cover us for claims arising from your content, your claims about the product, your failure to disclose, or your breach of these terms.
