## Your first 3 nights

**Every night:** 15 minutes, beside your child. Praise the move, not the answer.

**Night 1 · Hold it, read it, set 1 to 9**
Push all beads away from the beam. Thumb pushes beads up. Pointer finger pushes them down. Bottom beads are 1. The top bead is 5. A bead counts when it touches the beam. Set 1 to 9 out loud. Stuck? Ask, "Free beads, or friend?" Then wait.

**Night 2 · Two-digit numbers**
Left rod is tens. Right rod is ones. Set 23, 47 and 85. Read each one back.

**Night 3 · The first friend move**
Set 4. Add 1. No free bead! Bring the 5 down and send 4 away. Plus 1 is plus 5, take away 4. Not yet? That's fine. Stage 2 teaches it.

**Night 4:** Lesson 1 is in your parent account. Sign in at childphenoms.com with the email you ordered with. Questions? hello@childphenoms.com
