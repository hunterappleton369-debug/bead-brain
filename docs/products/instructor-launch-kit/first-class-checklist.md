# First-class checklist

Everything to do for class 1, in the order you do it: the weeks before, the day itself, and the day after. Tick each box. Most problems in a first class come from something that was not set up the week before.

## Three weeks before

- [ ] You have passed the certification test and have your badge and instructor login.
- [ ] Pick a day, time and place. Same slot every week for eight weeks. Check the eight dates against school holidays.
- [ ] Choose your price options from `pricing-sheet.md` and write them down.
- [ ] Fill in and print the flyer (`parent-flyer.md`, Version A). Ask the school office before you hand any out.
- [ ] Post Version B in one or two parent groups you already belong to.
- [ ] Learn the pickup script (`sign-up-script.md`) well enough to say it in your own words.
- [ ] Teach the week 1 and week 2 lessons to your own child, or to a friend, once. You will find the slow spots before the class does.

## Two weeks before

- [ ] Send the enrolment message to every family who says yes. Collect payment before class 1.
- [ ] Send each family the intake form (`parent-intake-form.md`). Chase any that are missing.
- [ ] Ask every family to order the free kit now (`childphenoms.com/free-abacus`). Shipping takes 2 to 3 business days and can run to a week.
- [ ] Order one or two spare sorobans for kids whose kit is late. `[HUNTER: is there a class-set or instructor price for spare sorobans?]`
- [ ] Set up a profile for each child on your instructor account. Write down each login.

## The week before

- [ ] Read through the intake forms. Note allergies, who may pick up each child, and photo consent. Keep this list with you at every class.
- [ ] Print the week 1 page from `class-drills.md`, one per child, plus your copy with the answers.
- [ ] Test the screen in the room. Open the screen abacus and the flash trainer from your account and check that kids at the back can see the beads.
- [ ] Read week 1 in `class-plans.md` twice. Time yourself on the lesson part: it should take 12 minutes, not 20.
- [ ] Send each family their child's app login.
- [ ] Send the day-before text (in `sign-up-script.md`) the day before.

## In your bag on the day

- [ ] Sorobans: the spares, plus your own
- [ ] Drill pages (one per child, plus yours)
- [ ] Pencils, a whiteboard and markers
- [ ] A timer (a phone is fine)
- [ ] Stickers
- [ ] The intake list (allergies, pickup, photo consent)
- [ ] Name tags
- [ ] Laptop and cables, charged

## One hour before

- [ ] Set up the room: kids in a half-circle facing the screen, a soroban and a page at each seat.
- [ ] Open the screen abacus. Clear it.
- [ ] Write the room rules on the board in one line each: thumb up, pointer down. Left to right. Say the move. Finish before you check.
- [ ] Put the parent note for week 1 in your phone, ready to send.

## During class 1

- [ ] Greet each child by name at the door. Check the name against your list.
- [ ] Keep to the 45-minute plan: warm-up 5, lesson 12, drill 13, flash game 8, wrap-up 7.
- [ ] Watch fingers more than answers in week 1. Fix the thumb and pointer kindly, every time.
- [ ] Write each child's Drill B score on the board.
- [ ] End on the flash game while it is still fun.
- [ ] Hand each child to an adult on their pickup list. Never to anyone else.

## Child safety (every class)

- Keep the door open, or have a second adult in the room. Never be alone with one child out of sight of others.
- Take photos or video only of children whose parents said yes on the intake form. Never post a photo that shows a child's school name or last name.
- Keep the intake forms private. Do not share a child's details with anyone outside the family.
- Know where the first-aid kit is. Know the address of the room so you can give it to 911.

## Within 24 hours after class 1

- [ ] Send the week 1 parent note to every family.
- [ ] Note each child's Drill B score in your own records.
- [ ] Check that every child has logged in to the app at least once. Text any family that has not.
- [ ] Write down one thing to change for class 2. Most new instructors talk too long in the lesson. Cut it, and give the time to the drill.
- [ ] Print the week 2 page.
