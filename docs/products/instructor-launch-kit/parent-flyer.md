# Parent flyer

Two versions of one flyer: a printed page for school pickup, and a post for parent groups online. Both follow Hook, Story, Offer. Fill in every `[BRACKET]` before you print or post.

## Before you fill it in

- **Only write what is true.** If your child has done the method at home, say what they can do now, in one line. If they have not started yet, cut that line. Never quote another parent unless they said it to you and said you can use it.
- **No promises about grades or speed.** Say what the class covers. Let the kids show the rest.
- **Use the badge** (see `directory-listing.md`) once, near your name. Not as the headline.

## Print and post

- Print Version A on white Letter paper. Render it with `npx md-to-pdf parent-flyer.md` (MIT, https://github.com/simonhaenisch/md-to-pdf), or hand the copy and the art notes to a designer.
- Ask the school office before you put a flyer up or in a backpack. Many schools have a rule.
- For Version B, check the group's rules on ads first. Post once. Answer every comment the same day.
