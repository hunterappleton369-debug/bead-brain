# Class plans: the 8-week starter course

Eight 45-minute classes for a group of four to eight kids, ages 7 to 11. They cover Stage 1 (Sprout) and Stage 2 (Explorer), the same lessons your students meet in the app.

## How the course is built

Proven abacus schools run on the same pattern. UCMAS, for one, pairs a weekly class with 15 to 20 minutes of practice at home every day, starts mental work almost at once, and grades accuracy before speed ([ucmas.com](https://www.ucmas.com/)). This course copies that shape at a smaller size.

- **One class a week, 45 minutes.** The class teaches the move and makes it fun to practise in a group.
- **Fifteen minutes a day at home.** The child practises on the app under your instructor account, with a parent sitting beside. Home practice is where the skill is built. The class is where it is started and checked.
- **Two app lessons per class.** Each week maps to two lessons in `js/curriculum.js`, in order. A child who misses a class can catch up at home on the same two lessons.
- **A boss test at week 4 and week 8.** These match the Sprout Boss and the Explorer Boss in the app. A child who does not pass keeps going and retakes it at home. Nobody is held back from the next class.

Every problem on the drill pages comes from `class-drills.md`. Those were made by the site's own generator and checked against the moves taught that week, so no child meets a move they have not learned yet. Never make up problems on the board. A made-up problem in week 3 can quietly need a week 5 move, and the child who gets it wrong learns the wrong lesson.

## The 45 minutes, every week

| Block | Minutes | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Warm-up | 5 | Quick group work on last week's move. Everyone answers together. |
| Lesson | 12 | You teach one or two moves on the screen abacus, then on real beads. |
| Drill | 13 | The printed page, in pairs or alone. You walk the room. |
| Flash game | 8 | The group game: Bead Peek, Eyes Closed or the Flash Round. |
| Wrap-up | 7 | Scores on the board, home plan, sticker, the parent note goes out. |

Keep to the clock. A class that runs long on the lesson loses the flash game, and the flash game is the part kids talk about in the car.

## Room rules (say them in week 1, then point to them)

Say these as plain sentences, not as a poster of rules. Kids remember a reason better than a rule.

The thumb pushes beads up and the pointer finger pushes them down, because only those two fingers are fast enough later. We work left to right, because that is how we say a number and how we will add in our heads. We say the move out loud, because the words teach the hand until the hand does not need them. And we finish the problem before we check, because checking halfway trains the eyes to look for help.

## Running a mixed group

Kids in the same class will move at different speeds by week 3. That is normal. Here is how to keep everyone working.

- **Pairs for drills.** Pair a faster child with a steady one. One sets, one reads, then they swap. The faster child slows down to explain, which is good practice for them too.
- **The fast finisher card.** A child who finishes the drill early times a second run of the same page and tries to beat their own time without dropping a point. Never hand them next week's move.
- **The stuck child.** Do not give the answer. Ask the week's question ("free beads, or friend?") and wait. If they are still stuck, show the move once on the screen abacus, clear it, and let them do it on their own beads.
- **The child who missed a class.** They do the two app lessons at home before the next class. In class they work the drill page with a partner and catch up in the flash game, which needs no new move.

## Week 1: Meet the beads

**App lessons:** Sprout 1, *Hold it, clear it, move it*, and Sprout 2, *Read any number*.
**By the end, each child can** clear the soroban, set any number from 0 to 9 with the right fingers, and read a two-digit number out loud.

**Warm-up (5 min).** Names and the room rules. Hand out the sorobans. Let them push beads for one minute, then teach the clear: tilt the top edge up so the beads slide away from the beam, lay it flat, and run the pointer finger along the top of the beam. Do the warm-up list on the drill page: you call a number, everyone sets it on one rod and clears.

**Lesson (12 min).** On the screen abacus, show the beam, the one heaven bead worth 5 above it and the four earth beads worth 1 below it. A bead counts only when it touches the beam. Show 1, 2, 3 and 4, then 5 as the heaven bead alone, then 6 as heaven plus one earth bead. Have the group set each one with you.

Then reading. Set 47 on the screen. Read the ones rod, then the tens, then say the whole number. Set 305 and point at the empty tens rod: a zero in the middle still takes up a place. That is the slip they will make most this week.

**Drill (13 min).** Week 1 page. Drill A alone: set each number, partner checks. Drill B in pairs: one sets a number from the list, the other reads it out loud. Swap after five.

**Flash game: Bead Peek (8 min).** Show each number from the flash list on the screen abacus for two seconds, then cover it. The group says it together. Then pick one child to say it alone. Cheer the fast correct ones, and shrug off the misses.

**Wrap-up (7 min).** Write each child's best Drill B score on the board. Set up the home routine: ten minutes a day, Sprout lessons 1 and 2 in the app, a parent beside.

**Parent note**

> Today your child met the beads. Ask them to show you 7. Thumb pushes up, pointer pushes down. Do 10 minutes a day in the app. Stop while it is still fun.

## Week 2: Read and set hundreds

**App lessons:** Sprout 3, *Read hundreds in a glance*, and Sprout 4, *Set numbers fast*.
**By the end, each child can** read a three-digit number in under three seconds and set it from the left.

**Warm-up (5 min).** Week 2 warm-up list. Show each two-digit number on the screen, the group says it together.

**Lesson (12 min).** Reading faster means not counting. Teach the look of each digit: 7 is "heaven plus two", 4 is "four up, heaven off". Set 694 on the screen and have them say it in one breath. Then flip it: you say a number, they set it from the left, hundreds, then tens, then ones. Left to right is the order they will add in later, so the habit starts now.

Watch the fingers. A child using the pointer to push earth beads up will be slow by week 5. Fix it now, kindly, every time.

**Drill (13 min).** Week 2 page. Drill A in pairs: one sets, the other reads in under three seconds. Drill B alone: set from the left while you call the numbers once each.

**Flash game: Bead Peek, hundreds (8 min).** Same game as last week, three-digit numbers, two seconds each. When the group is getting four of six, try one second.

**Wrap-up (7 min).** Board scores. Home plan: Sprout lessons 3 and 4, plus the daily quest "Read 3-digit numbers".

**Parent note**

> This week your child learned to read big numbers on the beads. Set a number like 694 and cover it after 2 seconds. Can they say it? Keep it to 10 minutes a day.

## Week 3: Add and take away when the beads are free

**App lessons:** Sprout 5, *Add when the beads are free*, and Sprout 6, *Take away when the beads are there*.
**By the end, each child can** add or take away on one rod whenever the beads they need are free.

**Warm-up (5 min).** Dictation. Call each three-digit number on the warm-up list once, at a steady pace. The group sets it. No repeats.

**Lesson (12 min).** To add, push more beads to the beam. If the beads you need are sitting free, move them. That is a direct add. Show 3 + 1, 1 + 5 and 6 + 3 on the screen. To take away, push beads off the beam. Show 8 − 3, 7 − 5 and 9 − 4.

Then plant the next idea. Set 4 and ask the group to add 1. No earth bead is free. Tell them there is a trick for that, and they learn it in week 5. Kids like being told a secret is coming.

**Drill (13 min).** Week 3 page. Drill A and B alone. Each problem lives on one rod: set the first number, then add or take away the second.

**Flash game: the first Flash Round (8 min).** Say three small numbers, one a second. The group moves the beads in their heads and calls out the total. All five rounds on the flash list need only free-bead moves. Say each number once. If a round falls apart, let it go and start fresh on the next.

**Wrap-up (7 min).** Board scores. Home plan: Sprout lessons 5 and 6, and the daily quest "Direct sums".

**Parent note**

> Your child can now add and take away on the beads. Say three small numbers, like 1, 6, 2. Can they give you the total? Short and daily beats long and weekly.

## Week 4: Two columns, eyes closed, and the Sprout Boss

**App lessons:** Sprout 7, *Two columns, left to right*, and Sprout 8, *Eyes closed*. Then a practice run of the Sprout Boss.
**By the end, each child can** work a three-line, two-digit problem left to right, and hold a one-digit picture with eyes shut.

**Warm-up (5 min).** Read three-digit numbers fast from the warm-up list.

**Lesson (8 min, short this week).** Bigger numbers are just more rods. Add the tens first, then the ones. Show 21 + 36 and 57 − 15 on the screen. This feels backwards to any child taught column addition at school, so say why: we add the number the same way we say it, and that is what makes math in the head possible later.

Then eyes closed. Set a number, let them look for one second, close their eyes, and say it. The picture in their head is the mental abacus. Tell them it is tiny now and every stage makes it bigger.

**Drill (10 min).** Week 4 page, Drill A alone.

**Flash game: Eyes Closed (5 min).** Show each number for one second, eyes shut, say it.

**Sprout Boss practice (10 min).** Run the three parts on the page, timed: read six hundreds in under a minute, set five in a minute and a half, then six problems in four minutes. The pass marks match the app's test. Kids who pass take the real Sprout Boss in the app at home this week. Kids who do not pass keep doing the daily quests and try again. Nobody is held back from week 5.

**Wrap-up (7 min).** Badges for anyone who passed a part. Fill in the week-4 progress report (`progress-report-template.md`) tonight and send it within two days.

**Parent note**

> Big week. Your child did two-digit sums and tried the Sprout Boss. Your report comes in 2 days. At home, ask them to "see" a number with eyes closed.

## Week 5: Small friends for adding

**App lessons:** Explorer 1, *+1 and +4: friends 1 and 4*, and Explorer 2, *+2 and +3: friends 2 and 3*.
**By the end, each child can** add 1, 2, 3 or 4 on a rod when the earth beads are not free, using the small friends.

**Warm-up (5 min).** Free-bead problems on one rod, called out loud. This reminds them what "free" means before you teach what to do when it is not.

**Lesson (12 min).** Here is the secret from week 3. Two numbers that make 5 are small friends: 1 and 4, 2 and 3. Set 4 and try to add 1. No earth bead is free. So bring the heaven bead down (+5) and push 4 earth beads away (−4). Plus 1 is plus 5, minus 4. Have the group say it as they move.

Show 3 + 4 (plus 5, minus 1), then 3 + 2 (plus 5, minus 3) and 4 + 3 (plus 5, minus 2). Write the four adding formulas on the board in orange, the small-friend colour in the app.

From now on every problem starts with one question: free beads, or friend? Make them say it before they move.

**Drill (13 min).** Week 5 page. Three problems in each drill are free-bead moves on purpose, so the child has to decide every time. That choice is the skill.

**Flash game: Flash Round (8 min).** Three small numbers, free beads only. The flash game stays one step behind the lesson on purpose, so it stays fast and fun.

**Wrap-up (7 min).** Board scores. Home plan: Explorer lessons 1 and 2, plus the daily quest "Friend or free?".

**Parent note**

> Your child learned the small friends today: 1 and 4, 2 and 3. If they get stuck, ask "free beads, or friend?" and wait. Do not touch the beads.

## Week 6: Small friends in real problems, and taking away

**App lessons:** Explorer 3, *Small-friend adds in 2-digit numbers*, and Explorer 4, *−1 and −4: taking away with friends*.
**By the end, each child can** use the adding friends inside four-line, two-digit problems, and take away 1 or 4 with a friend.

**Warm-up (5 min).** Friend or free, adding only. Call each problem, the group answers and says the move.

**Lesson (12 min).** First, the friends inside bigger problems. Show 23 + 32: the tens are direct, the ones need a friend. Then 41 + 14: the tens need a friend, the ones are direct. Ask the question on every rod.

Then taking away. Set 5 and try to take away 1. No earth bead is on the beam. So push the heaven bead up (−5) and bring 4 earth beads up (+4). Minus 1 is minus 5, plus 4. Show 6 − 4 (minus 5, plus 1) and 8 − 4.

**Drill (13 min).** Week 6 page. Drill A in pairs: one works the problem, the other listens for the friend said out loud, then swap. Drill B alone.

**Flash game: Flash Round, plus and minus (8 min).** Four small numbers, some of them take-aways. Say "minus six" as one chunk. A child who hears "minus" and then waits for the number has already lost the beat.

**Wrap-up (7 min).** Board scores. Home plan: Explorer lessons 3 and 4.

**Parent note**

> Today your child used small friends in bigger sums, and started taking away with them. Praise the move they say out loud, not just the right answer.

## Week 7: All eight small friends

**App lessons:** Explorer 5, *−2 and −3*, and Explorer 6, *All eight friends, mixed*.
**By the end, each child can** use all eight small-friend moves in four-line, two-digit problems, both to add and to take away.

**Warm-up (5 min).** Friend or free, take-aways. Call each problem, the group answers.

**Lesson (12 min).** Minus 2 is minus 5, plus 3. Minus 3 is minus 5, plus 2. Same move, different friend. Show 6 − 2, 5 − 3 and 7 − 3. Then put all eight formulas on the board and run a mixed problem together on the screen, rod by rod, with the group calling the move.

Tell them one more thing: none of these problems go past 9 or below 0 on a rod. That is the next stage, and it needs the big friends.

**Drill (13 min).** Week 7 page. Drill A alone, Drill B in pairs.

**Flash game: Flash Round, two-digit (8 min).** Three two-digit numbers, one every two seconds. Each number lands on two rods at once, tens then ones. Slow is fine. Accuracy first, and the speed follows.

**Wrap-up (7 min).** Board scores. Invite families to next week's last class: the final 15 minutes is a family flash round. Home plan: Explorer lessons 5 and 6.

**Parent note**

> Your child now knows all 8 small friends. Next week is our last class. Come for the last 15 minutes and see a flash round. Bring a grandparent if you can.

## Week 8: Speed, the mental picture, and the Explorer Boss

**App lessons:** Explorer 7, *Speed round*, and Explorer 8, *Mental: three small numbers*. Then a practice run of the Explorer Boss.
**By the end, each child can** run a timed five-line page, add three small numbers on the mental beads, and attempt the Explorer Boss.

**Warm-up (5 min).** Friend or free, with plus and minus in the same list.

**Lesson (5 min).** Put the sorobans away. Picture one empty rod. Add the numbers on the beads in your head. If the picture fades, tap the table with the thumb and pointer finger as if the beads were there. Moving the fingers keeps the picture alive.

**Drill (12 min).** Week 8 page. Drill A is timed: everyone writes their time at the top. Drill B is mental, sorobans out of reach.

**Explorer Boss practice (8 min).** The three parts on the page, timed, with the app's pass marks. Kids who pass take the real Explorer Boss in the app at home. Kids who do not pass keep up the daily quests and retake it. Both are normal.

**Family flash round (15 min, families watching).** Seat the families at the back. Run the flash list on the page: four small numbers, plus and minus, one a second. Then invite a parent or grandparent to call a round, with one rule: one number at a time, said once. Let a child lose a round in front of people. They shrug and ask for another, and the adults watch the picture hold up under pressure. That moment sells the next course better than anything you can say.

Hand each family the week-8 progress report on the way out, or send it within two days. Offer the next course (Stage 3, Apprentice) in person while the families are in the room. Use `sign-up-script.md`.
