# Progress report template

The one-page report each family gets after week 4 and after week 8. Fill it in from your class notes and the app, then send it within two days.

## How to fill it in (for the instructor)

- **Use real numbers only.** Scores come from the drill pages and the app. If you did not record it, leave it out.
- **One strength, one next step.** Name a move, not a trait. "Sets three-digit numbers from the left without looking at her fingers" beats "She's so smart."
- **Praise the move said out loud** more than the right answer. That is the habit that carries into the next stage.
- **No comparisons** with other kids in the class.
- **Keep it short.** A parent should read it in one minute.
- Week 4 covers Stage 1 (Sprout). Week 8 covers Stage 2 (Explorer) and adds the next step.
