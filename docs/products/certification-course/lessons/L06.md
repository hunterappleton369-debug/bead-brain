# L06 · Small friends, taking away, and the Explorer boss

The same four moves run backwards, all eight mixed, the first mental round, and the second boss.

**Week** 2 · **Video** <!-- runtime:start -->about 5 min<!-- runtime:end --> · **Lesson time** 90 min · **Mirrors** Stage 2 lessons "−1 and −4", "−2 and −3", "All eight friends, mixed", "Speed round", "Mental: three small numbers" and the Explorer Boss

## Video script

[HUNTER: close-up on one rod, then a two-rod problem.]

You know the four small-friend adds. Today we run them backwards. It's easier than it sounds, because a child who owns a move forwards learns it backwards fast.

### Taking away with a friend

Set 5 on one rod. The heaven bead is down. No earth beads. Now take away 1.

Is there an earth bead on the beam to remove? No. So friend. Take away five, and give back its friend. Push the heaven bead up. That's minus five. Bring four earth beads up. That's plus four. The rod shows 4.

Minus one is minus five, add four. "Minus one: take away five, add four."

[on screen: −1 = −5 +4 · −2 = −5 +3 · −3 = −5 +2 · −4 = −5 +1]

Six take away four. Six is heaven plus one. Four earth beads on the beam? Only one. Friend. Heaven up, minus five. Add one, the friend of four. The rod shows 2.

Seven take away three. Heaven plus two. Not enough earth beads. Minus five, add two. The rod shows 4.

### When it happens

A small-friend take-away happens when the heaven bead is down and there aren't enough earth beads on the beam. If the whole rod is too small for the number, that's a borrow, and it comes in Lesson 8.

### The order the trainer uses

Minus one and minus four first. Then minus two and minus three. Then all eight mixed, in two-digit problems four lines long. Then a speed round: ten problems, five lines each, in five minutes.

The speed round is the first time a clock matters. Accuracy still comes first. The pass mark is eight of ten. A child who rushes and gets six has learned nothing about speed. A child who gets nine at a slow pace will be fast in two weeks.

### The first mental round

At the end of Stage 2, the soroban goes away for a few minutes. Three small numbers, read aloud, added in the head. Free beads only. Three, then five, then one. The child says nine.

[on screen: 3 · 5 · 1 → 9]

Keep the numbers inside the moves the child owns. In Stage 2, mental rounds use free beads only. The head can only use a move the hand already owns.

Say each number once, on a steady beat. One a second. Don't repeat it. Let it land and go. The child says nothing until the end. Just the total.

If a round falls apart, let it go. Next one. "Let it go. Next one." That line will save you a lot of tears.

### The Explorer boss

[on screen: Explorer Boss · Friend or free?: 10 moves, 2 min, 9 right · 2-digit, 4 lines: 6 problems, 4 min, 5 right · Mental: 5 problems, 2 min, 4 right]

Three parts. Part one: ten single-rod moves on the screen abacus, two minutes, no hints. Nine right. That part is pure decision. Free beads, or friend? Part two: six two-digit problems, four lines, on the soroban, four minutes. Five right. Part three: five mental problems, three small numbers each, two minutes. Four right.

### What stuck looks like at this stage

The child stares at the rod. The seconds stretch. Every instinct you have says "bring the five down." Don't.

Ask "Free beads, or friend?" and wait. Keep your hands in your lap. It sounds small. It's the hardest part of the job.

If a child forgets a formula in the first week of a new group, that's normal. Ask, "What's the friend of three?" If it lasts past a week, the problem isn't this formula. It's the group before it, which isn't automatic yet. Go back one lesson. Repeat its drill until it passes three days running. Then come forward.

### How long Explorer takes

About three to four weeks for most kids of seven or older. Some fly through the adds and stall on the take-aways. That's fine. The stall is the brain learning the move backwards.

### The parent line

Here's the one line you give the parent for Stage 2. "When they hesitate, ask 'free beads, or friend?' and wait. Don't touch the beads."

That's the whole parent job this month. Next lesson: the beads run out of room, and we carry.

## Teach-back

Run part three of the Explorer boss (the mental round) with a partner, using section C. Read at one number a second. Say each number once. Say nothing else until they answer. Then swap: have them read section C to you while you keep the total in your head. Write one sentence on what it felt like to be the child.

## Check: five questions

1. Write all four small-friend take-aways as formulas.
2. The rod shows 7. Take away 4. Free beads or friend? Give the move and the answer.
3. What are the three parts of the Explorer boss and their pass marks?
4. A child still forgets the small-friend take-aways after ten days. What do you do?
5. Why do Stage 2 mental rounds use free beads only?
