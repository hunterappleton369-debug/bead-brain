# L07 · Big friends: carrying

When a number won't fit on the rod, take away its big friend and carry one to the left. The stage where the soroban starts beating paper.

**Week** 2 · **Video** <!-- runtime:start -->about 5 min<!-- runtime:end --> · **Lesson time** 105 min · **Mirrors** Stage 3 (Apprentice) lessons "+9, +8, +7", "+6 and +5", "+4, +3, +2, +1 past nine" and "Carry chains"

## Video script

[HUNTER: two rods in close-up, ones and tens. Blue is the big-friend color in the trainer.]

A rod holds nine, at most. So what happens when the answer is bigger than nine?

Set 4 on the ones rod. Add 9. Four plus nine is thirteen. Thirteen won't fit on one rod. We need the tens rod.

This is carrying. And it uses a new kind of friend.

### Big friends make ten

Two numbers that make ten are big friends. One and nine. Two and eight. Three and seven. Four and six. Five and five.

[on screen: 1 + 9 · 2 + 8 · 3 + 7 · 4 + 6 · 5 + 5]

Here's the move. To add a number that won't fit, take away its big friend on this rod, and carry one to the rod on the left.

Back to four plus nine. The big friend of nine is one. Take one earth bead away from the ones rod. Now carry one: push one earth bead up on the tens rod. Tens shows 1, ones shows 3. Thirteen.

"Plus nine: take away one, carry one to the left."

[on screen: +9 = −1 +10]

### More of them

Seven plus eight. The friend of eight is two. Take away two. Carry one. Fifteen.

Eight plus seven. The friend of seven is three. Take away three. Carry one. Fifteen.

Plus five is the odd one. It happens when the heaven bead is already down and you need another five. Five plus five: take away five, carry one. Ten. Nine plus five: take away five, carry one. Fourteen.

[on screen: +9 = −1 +10 · +8 = −2 +10 · +7 = −3 +10 · +6 = −4 +10 · +5 = −5 +10]

### Small numbers past nine

Small numbers can fail to fit too, when the rod is nearly full. Nine plus one. No room. The friend of one is nine. Take away nine, carry one. Ten.

Eight plus three: take away seven, carry one. Eleven. Seven plus four: take away six, carry one. Eleven.

So there are nine big-friend adds, one for each number from one to nine. The trainer teaches them in three groups. Plus nine, plus eight, plus seven come first. Then six and five. Then four down to one.

### The four questions

Now the question on every rod grows. It was one question. Now it's three.

Are the beads free? If not, is there a small friend? If it won't fit on the rod, big friend.

[on screen: Free beads? · Small friend? · Won't fit? Big friend.]

The child asks these in order. When it's automatic, it takes no time at all. In the first weeks, it takes a lot. Let it.

### The order of the two moves

Take the friend away on this rod first. Then carry. Same order every time, for every child. It keeps the hand moving the same way, and it keeps the line and the hand in step.

### Carry chains

Sometimes the carry itself won't fit. Ninety-nine plus one. The ones rod says take away nine, carry one. But the tens rod holds nine too. So the carry is just another plus one, with the same rules. Take away nine, carry again. One hundred.

[on screen: 99 + 1 → 100]

And the carry can need a friend. Say the tens rod shows 4 and a carry arrives. Plus one on a rod showing 4. No free bead. That's a small friend: plus five, take away four. The carry follows the same questions as any other move.

That's what the "carry chains" lesson drills. Five lines of two-digit adds, and carries that land on full rods.

### Where the school habit comes back

Carrying is the one thing school did teach. And school taught it right to left, ones first. So this is the stage where the old habit creeps back. You'll see the eyes jump to the ones.

Insist on left to right, every time. Tell the parent the same. "Slower for two weeks, faster for life."

### The stage kids want to quit

Here's a warning for you as a teacher. Stage 3 is where many kids say "I want to quit." Carrying is new. The beads feel slower than fingers for a week or two.

Don't make a speech. Shorten the session. Drop to the daily quest alone if you need to. The feeling passes when the carries become automatic, and the soroban is suddenly faster than anything. We'll cover what to say in Lesson 19.

### How long Apprentice takes

About five to six weeks. It's a long stage. It's also the stage where five-line problems become the daily work, and the child starts beating adults with paper.

Next lesson: the same nine moves, backwards. Borrowing.

## Teach-back

Make a one-page "big friends" card for kids, in your own handwriting: the five pairs, the nine adds, and one worked example. Then teach "+9, +8, +7" to a partner in ten minutes using the card and section A. Afterward, list every word on your card a seven-year-old might not know, and replace it.
