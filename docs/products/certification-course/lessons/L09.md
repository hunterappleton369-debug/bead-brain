# L09 · Both friends at once: all 34 moves

The last eight moves, built from the first twenty-six. After this lesson, nothing new ever happens on a rod again.

**Week** 3 · **Video** <!-- runtime:start -->about 5 min<!-- runtime:end --> · **Lesson time** 105 min · **Mirrors** Stage 4 (Adept) lessons "Combo adds: 6+6, 7+7, 8+8, 5+9", "Combo take-aways: 11−6, 12−7, 13−8, 14−9", "All 34 formulas, mixed", "Three digits", "Ten rows" and the Adept Boss

## Video script

[HUNTER: one rod close-up for 6 + 6. Purple is the combination color in the trainer.]

Set 6 on the ones rod. Heaven bead down, one earth bead up. Now add 6.

Six won't fit. Six plus six is twelve. So it's a big friend. The big friend of six is four. Take away four, carry one.

But look at the rod. It shows the heaven bead and one earth bead. There's no four to take away.

This is the last kind of move. And you already know both halves of it.

### Both friends at once

We need to take away four, and four isn't there as earth beads. How do you take away four when the beads aren't there? Small friend. Minus four is minus five, plus one.

So put it together. Plus six is: take away five, add one, carry one.

[on screen: +6 = −5 +1 +10]

Push the heaven bead up. Bring one earth bead up. Carry one to the tens. The beads show 12.

"Plus six: take away five, add one, carry one." Three moves, one formula, said in one breath.

### The four adds

[on screen: +6 = −5 +1 +10 · +7 = −5 +2 +10 · +8 = −5 +3 +10 · +9 = −5 +4 +10]

Seven plus seven. The friend of seven is three, and three isn't there as earth beads. So take away five, add two, carry. Fourteen.

Five plus nine. Take away five, add four, carry. Fourteen.

Eight plus six. Take away five, add one, carry. Fourteen.

These only happen in one situation. You're adding six to nine onto a rod that shows five to eight, and the big friend isn't there to take away. The trainer finds those spots and gives the child the reps.

### The four take-aways

Eleven take away six. The ones rod shows 1. Six isn't there. So add the big friend of six, which is four, then borrow one from the tens. But the ones rod shows 1, and only three earth beads are free. Adding four needs a small friend: plus five, take away one.

So minus six is: add five, take away one, borrow one. The beads show 5.

[on screen: −6 = +5 −1 −10 · −7 = +5 −2 −10 · −8 = +5 −3 −10 · −9 = +5 −4 −10]

Twelve take away seven: add five, take away two, borrow. Five. Thirteen take away eight: add five, take away three, borrow. Five. Fourteen take away nine: add five, take away four, borrow. Five.

They happen when you take six to nine from a rod showing one to four, with a borrow.

### Thirty-four

Eight small friends. Eighteen big friends. Eight combinations. Thirty-four.

[on screen: 8 + 18 + 8 = 34]

Some programs count thirty-two. Same ground, counted a different way. Ours is thirty-four.

### The four questions, complete

Now the question on every rod is complete. Four steps, and it never gets a fifth.

Are the beads free? If not, is there a small friend? If it won't fit, big friend. If the friend isn't there, both at once.

[on screen: Free beads? → Small friend? → Won't fit: big friend → Friend not there: both]

### After the thirty-fourth move

When the last one is automatic, nothing new ever happens on a rod again.

Three digits? Three rods, same moves. Four hundred sixty-eight plus three hundred fifty-seven is hundreds, then tens, then ones, with the carries running across.

Ten rows instead of five? Same moves, more of them. The eyes stay one line ahead of the hand. The answer isn't checked until the end.

From here on, the whole game is fluency. And fluency is what lets the beads come off the table. A hand that doesn't have to think about the move leaves the mind free to hold the picture.

### The Adept boss

[on screen: Adept Boss · Combo moves: 10 in 150 s, 9 right · 3-digit, 5 lines: 6 problems, 6 min, 5 right · Mental: 5 problems of three 2-digit numbers, 3 min, 4 right]

Part one: ten combination moves on the screen abacus, two and a half minutes, no hints. Nine right. Part two: six problems, three digits, five lines, six minutes. Five right. Part three: five mental problems, three two-digit numbers added. Four right.

That third part means the child is adding two-digit numbers in their head. Most adults stop near there. We cover the mental lessons properly in Lesson 17.

### How long Adept takes

About five to six weeks. Sprout to Adept, the first four stages, is roughly the ground the big programs call their foundation levels.

### The parent line

"Praise the formula said out loud more than the right answer." A child who says "plus seven: take away five, add two, carry one" is a child whose hand is about to do it right.

Next lesson, we step back from the beads. We look at how to teach all thirty-four in the right order, and how to ask the one question that does the teaching for you.
