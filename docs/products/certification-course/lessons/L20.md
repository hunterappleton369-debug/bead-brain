# L20 · Your first class, the test, and the Launch Kit

How the certification test works, what the Launch Kit gives you, and how to run your first class without making a single promise you can't keep.

## Video script

[HUNTER: talking head. Have the printed Launch Kit pages on the table to hold up.]

This is the last lesson. Three things today. The test. The kit. And your first class.

### The test

The certification test has forty questions in three parts.

[on screen: Part A · 12 bead moves · Part B · 14 worked problems · Part C · 14 teaching scenarios]

Part A is twelve bead moves. A rod shows a number, you add or take away. You name the move and say its line. That's Lesson 10's drill, exactly.

Part B is fourteen worked problems. Stacked sums from every stage, multiplication, division, one mental sum, and two problems where you name every rod move in order.

Part C is fourteen teaching scenarios. A child is stuck. A parent asks a question. You pick what a certified instructor says or does.

You have sixty minutes. You can use your soroban for Parts A and B, except the one mental problem.

To pass, you need thirty-two of forty. That's eighty percent. And no part can fall below seventy percent. Part C matters most to me. You can be a little slow on the beads. You can't tell a parent the beads raise IQ.

### Take it when you're ready

The test is on demand. Take it when the drills in this course feel calm, not before.

If you don't pass, you get your scores by part. Retake it whenever you're ready, as many times as you need. A fresh set of worked problems is made for every retake.

### The Pass Promise

And if you don't pass, we coach you one on one until you do. That's the Pass Promise. Here's how it runs. After a second miss, or any time you ask, you book a coaching call. We look at which part missed. We give you the two or three lessons to redo, and a short drill. Then you retake. As many rounds as it takes.

You also have thirty days to change your mind about the course, for a full refund.

### The Launch Kit

When you pass, you get the Launch Kit.

[on screen: Class plans · Pricing sheet · Parent flyer · Sign-up script · First-class checklist]

Class plans: eight weeks of forty-five-minute classes for Sprout and Explorer, written out. Lesson 12's six blocks, with every drill named.

A pricing sheet. Here's the only thing I'll say about money. Most instructors land between twenty and forty dollars a session. Your rates are yours to set. Some people teach for free, for their own kids and their friends. That's up to you. Nobody can promise you an income, and I won't.

A parent flyer, in two versions, one for school pickup and one for online.

A sign-up script, for the conversation at pickup, with answers for the three wrong beliefs from Lesson 1.

And a first-class checklist.

You also get your certificate and the instructor badge for your flyer. [HUNTER: the Certified Instructor directory page does not exist yet. Say when listings go live, or cut this line before recording.]

### Your first class

Start with kids you know. Your own child, and two or three friends from school. Most instructors start in person.

[on screen: 4–8 kids · same stage · 45 minutes · Sprout]

Keep the group small, four to eight kids, all starting at Sprout. Use the first plan in the kit as written. Don't improve it yet. Run it once as written, then change one thing.

Before the class, play the two-minute talk you recorded in Lesson 1. That's what you'll say to the parents at the door. Promise, your story, the one thing, three secrets. And only what you can back up.

### Keeping kids safe

You're working with other people's children. Keep a parent in the room, or the door open. Never be alone with one child. Online, the parent stays in the room with the camera on. [HUNTER: decide whether instructors need a background check. The legal-and-ops folder has the instructor agreement.]

### Between classes

The class teaches the move. The home minutes make it automatic. Every family needs the trainer for the fifteen minutes a day at home. Send each family to the free kit and the University, so their child practices the exact lessons you teach.

Your instructor account comes with a year of the software for your own child, with unlimited student profiles. It renews at the member rate after the first year. The certification itself is yours for life.

### One last thing

When I learned this, I had no coach. I guessed my way through it. You won't have to. And the kids in your class won't have to either.

Go take the test. I'll see you on the other side.
