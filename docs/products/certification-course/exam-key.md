# Certified Phenom Instructor exam: answer key

Marker's copy of the key for `exam.md`. Keep it off any page a candidate can see.

## Marking rules

- One point per question. No half points.
- **Part A.** The point needs the right group (free beads, small friend, big friend, or both friends at once) **and** the right formula. The spoken line can be in the candidate's own words if it names the same beads. For borrows, the house order is the friend first, then the borrow ("Minus 8: add 2, borrow 1 from the left"), as Lesson 8 teaches. A candidate who names both moves in the other order still gets the beads right and loses no point; coach the house order in the feedback.
- **Part B.** The exact answer. For division with a remainder, both the quotient and the remainder. For 25 and 26, every rod move in the right order and the right answer.
- **Part C.** The letter below only.
- **Pass.** 32 of 40 or more, **and** Part A at least 9 of 12, Part B at least 10 of 14, Part C at least 10 of 14.
- **Feedback.** Send the candidate their score for each part, and for every missed question the lesson it comes from (Part A: Lessons 5–10, Part B: the lesson for that operation, Part C: the lesson named below). Never send this key.
