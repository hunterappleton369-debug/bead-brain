# Certified Phenom Instructor: syllabus

Twenty lessons and one exam over six weeks, about 30 hours in all, at your own pace.

## How the course runs

Each lesson has four parts. A short video in Hunter's voice. A drill set you work on your own soroban, with answers at the bottom of the lesson. A teach-back task, where you teach the lesson to a partner or a child and note what happened. And a five-question check.

Lesson time below covers all four parts. Video times are read times for the script, and the on-screen demos add a little. The pace is a guide. Most weeks are about five hours. Go faster or slower as your week allows.

| Week | Lessons | Theme | Hours |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | L01–L04 | Why it works, the tool, direct moves, the first boss | 5.0 |
| 2 | L05–L07 | Small friends and carrying | 4.75 |
| 3 | L08–L10 | Borrowing, all 34 moves, teaching the order | 4.75 |
| 4 | L11–L14 | Drill types, sessions and classes, bosses, the Bead Score | 5.5 |
| 5 | L15–L17 | Multiplication, division, the mental picture | 5.0 |
| 6 | L18–L20 + exam | Flash, stuck moments, your first class, the test | 5.0 |
| | | **Total** | **30.0** |

## Week 1 · Foundations

### L01 · Why this works, and what you may promise
- **Video** about 7 min · **Lesson time** 45 min.
- **You learn** the four-part parent talk (promise, story, the one thing, three secrets), what the research found and did not find, and every claim you may and may never make.
- **Drill homework** sort twelve parent-facing lines into "say" and "never", and rewrite the "never" lines.
- **Teach-back** record your own two-minute parent talk and check it against the "never" list.
- **Mirrors** Playbook chapters 1 and 3 and the science appendix.
.
### L02 · The soroban, the hand, reading and setting
- **Video** about 5 min · **Lesson time** 90 min.
- **You learn** the parts of the soroban, clearing, the thumb and index split, bead shapes, reading in under three seconds, setting from the left, and the eyes-closed drill.
- **Drill homework** read 20 numbers (2 and 3 digits) and set 10.
- **Mirrors** Stage 1 lessons "Hold it, clear it, move it", "Read any number", "Read hundreds in a glance", "Set numbers fast", "Eyes closed".
.
### L03 · Direct moves: when the beads are free
- **Video** about 5 min · **Lesson time** 75 min.
- **You learn** direct adds and take-aways, the "are the beads free?" check, and how to coach with questions only.
- **Drill homework** 20 single-rod direct moves.
- **Mirrors** Stage 1 lessons "Add when the beads are free", "Take away when the beads are there".
.
### L04 · Two columns, left to right, and the first boss
- **Video** about 5 min · **Lesson time** 90 min.
- **You learn** how a stacked problem runs, why left to right, and the Sprout boss and its pass marks.
- **Drill homework** 10 mixed direct moves and 10 two-digit, three-line problems.
- **Mirrors** Stage 1 lesson "Two columns, left to right" and the Sprout Boss.
.
## Week 2 · Small friends and carrying

### L05 · Small friends, adding
- **Video** about 5 min · **Lesson time** 90 min.
- **You learn** the four small-friend adds, when they happen, teaching them in pairs, and the question "free beads, or friend?".
- **Drill homework** 20 single-rod moves with decoys and 8 two-digit, four-line problems.
- **Mirrors** Stage 2 lessons "+1 and +4", "+2 and +3", "Small-friend adds in 2-digit numbers".
.
### L06 · Small friends, taking away, and the Explorer boss
- **Video** about 5 min · **Lesson time** 90 min.
- **You learn** the four small-friend take-aways, the speed round, the first mental round, and the Explorer boss.
- **Drill homework** 10 single-rod take-aways, 8 mixed four-line problems, 5 mental rounds.
- **Mirrors** Stage 2 lessons "−1 and −4", "−2 and −3", "All eight friends, mixed", "Speed round", "Mental: three small numbers", the Explorer Boss.
.
### L07 · Big friends: carrying
- **Video** about 5 min · **Lesson time** 105 min.
- **You learn** the nine big-friend adds, the order of the two moves, carry chains, and the week kids want to quit.
- **Drill homework** 12 carry moves (naming every rod) and 6 five-line carry chains.
- **Mirrors** Stage 3 lessons "+9, +8, +7", "+6 and +5", "+4, +3, +2, +1 past nine", "Carry chains".
.
## Week 3 · Borrowing and all 34

### L08 · Big friends: borrowing, and the Apprentice boss
- **Video** about 5 min · **Lesson time** 105 min.
- **You learn** the nine big-friend take-aways (add the friend, then borrow), borrow chains, mixed problems, the first mental crossing of ten, and the Apprentice boss.
- **Drill homework** 12 borrow moves, 6 mixed five-line problems, 5 mental rounds.
- **Mirrors** Stage 3 lessons "−9, −8, −7", "−6 down to −1", "Borrow chains and mixed problems", "Mental: small friends in the head", "Mental: crossing ten", the Apprentice Boss.
.
### L09 · Both friends at once: all 34 moves
- **Video** about 6 min · **Lesson time** 105 min.
- **You learn** the eight combination moves, the full four-question check, three digits, ten rows, and the Adept boss.
- **Drill homework** 12 combination moves, 5 six-line problems, 4 three-digit problems, 2 ten-row problems.
- **Mirrors** Stage 4 lessons "Combo adds", "Combo take-aways", "All 34 formulas, mixed", "Three digits", "Ten rows", the Adept Boss.
.
### L10 · Teaching the order, and the one question
- **Video** about 6 min · **Lesson time** 75 min.
- **You learn** why the 34 moves come in this order, when a child is ready for the next group, never giving the answer, the colors, and praise that names a move.
- **Drill homework** name the move for 20 single-rod problems (the skill Part A of the exam tests).
- **Mirrors** Stages 2 to 4 as a whole, and Playbook chapters 4 and 8.
.
## Week 4 · Running sessions

### L11 · Drill types and tempo
- **Video** about 6 min · **Lesson time** 90 min.
- **You learn** the five drill families every proven program uses, how our stages map onto the UCMAS modules, how to read numbers aloud, and when to speed up.
- **Drill homework** one set of each drill type: written, dictation, flash, mental.
- **Mirrors** the trainer's drill kinds and Stage 5 lessons "Flash anzan begins", "Listening: numbers read aloud".
.
### L12 · Running a 15-minute session and a 45-minute class
- **Video** about 6 min · **Lesson time** 90 min.
- **You learn** the daily routine, the weekly rhythm, a six-block class plan, mixed levels, and teaching online.
- **Drill homework** run a ready-made 15-minute session pack, then time yourself on 3-digit, ten-line problems.
- **Mirrors** the trainer's daily routine, Stage 5 lessons "Fast hands", "Read ahead", and the Launch Kit class plans.
.
### L13 · Boss tests, pass marks, and when to hold a child back
- **Video** about 6 min · **Lesson time** 75 min.
- **You learn** all ten bosses, how pass marks are set, how to read a failed part, when to hold a child back, and how to say "not yet" to a parent.
- **Drill homework** mark two practice boss sheets and give a pass or not-yet verdict.
- **Mirrors** the boss test at the end of every stage.
.
### L14 · The Bead Score and the kyu ladder
- **Video** about 6 min · **Lesson time** 75 min.
- **You learn** how the 20-rung test runs, the rungs that matter, the three Japanese ladders (bead, anzan, flash), why we always say "about", and the age chart.
- **Drill homework** take your own Bead Score, rungs 1 to 6.
- **Mirrors** Stage 5 lesson "Your first Bead Score", the Bead Score and Times ladders, Playbook chapter 7.
.
## Week 5 · Multiply and divide, then the picture

### L15 · Multiplication on the beads
- **Video** about 5 min · **Lesson time** 105 min.
- **You learn** the times-table gate, the two-digit product rule, where each product lands, and mental 2-digit × 1-digit.
- **Drill homework** 24 problems from 2 × 1 up to 3 × 2, plus 6 mental.
- **Mirrors** Stage 6 (Wizard), all lessons and the Wizard Boss.
.
### L16 · Division on the beads
- **Video** about 5 min · **Lesson time** 105 min.
- **You learn** guess, multiply, take away; remainders; dividing by two digits; what to do with a guess that is too big.
- **Drill homework** 25 division problems, including remainders and 2-digit divisors, plus 5 mental.
- **Mirrors** Stage 7 (Champion), all lessons and the Champion Boss.
.
### L17 · Anzan: building the picture
- **Video** about 6 min · **Lesson time** 90 min.
- **You learn** what the research says about the mental picture, the three-to-four rod limit, how the picture grows stage by stage, and the fix when it collapses.
- **Drill homework** 22 mental rounds from one rod up to three.
- **Mirrors** the mental lessons in Stages 1 to 9.
.
## Week 6 · Flash, stuck moments, and launch

### L18 · Flash anzan: tempos and the kyu table
- **Video** about 5 min · **Lesson time** 90 min.
- **You learn** where flash came from, the flash anzan grades, the trainer's flash tempos by stage, flash at the table, and the grandparent show.
- **Drill homework** 23 flash rounds from three numbers at the table up to five 3-digit numbers.
- **Mirrors** the flash lessons in Stages 5, 7, 8, 9 and 10, and Playbook chapter 6.
.
### L19 · Common mistakes, and what to say
- **Video** about 6 min · **Lesson time** 75 min.
- **You learn** the three stuck lines, how to read a mistake from the size of the miss, the line for each stuck moment, and the three class problems.
- **Drill homework** diagnose 8 worked problems with planted errors.
- **Mirrors** Playbook chapter 8 and the trainer's stuck help.
.
### L20 · Your first class, the test, and the Launch Kit
- **Video** about 5 min · **Lesson time** 75 min.
- **You learn** how the exam and the Pass Promise work, what is in the Launch Kit, your first class, and child safety.
- **Drill homework** a 10-problem mixed review, one of each kind on the exam.
- **Mirrors** the exam, the Launch Kit and the Pass Promise.
.
### The exam
- **Time** 60 minutes.
- 40 questions: 12 bead moves, 14 worked problems, 14 teaching scenarios. Pass with 32 of 40 (80%), with no part below its floor. Retake on demand. See `exam.md` and `pass-promise-ops.md`.
.
## Totals

- 20 lessons, about 110 minutes of video script, 29 hours of lesson time, and a 1-hour exam: **30 hours**.
- Six weeks at about five hours a week.
.
