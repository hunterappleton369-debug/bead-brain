# Child Prodigy Secrets: The Playbook — audiobook recording guide

One page for whoever records it. The audiobook is the Playbook read aloud by Hunter, start to finish, for the school run. The Workbook is not recorded; it is a thing you do with a pencil.

## Tone

Hunter, to one parent, at a kitchen table. Not a narrator. The first chapter is a confession and should sound like one: slower, plainer, no selling. From chapter 2 on it is a coach who has run this a hundred times: warm, certain, unhurried. Sentences are short on purpose; let them land, do not rush to the next one. Smile on the grandparent lines.

Pacing: 140–155 words a minute. Pause a full beat at every section break and two beats at a chapter break. The spoken formulas ("Plus three: add five, take away two") are said the way you would say them to a child beside you, with the colon as a breath.

## What to do with the tables

- **Chapter 4, small friends (8 rows).** Read the whole table, adds then take-aways, in rhythm: "Plus one is plus five, take away four. Plus two is plus five, take away three…" It is short and it is the heart of the book.
- **Chapter 4, big friends (18 rows).** Do not read all eighteen. Say: "The table in the book runs from plus nine, take away one and carry, down to plus one, take away nine and carry, and the take-aways mirror it. The card at the back of the book has all eighteen." Then read the three worked examples in the prose.
- **Chapter 4, combinations (8 rows).** Read all eight; they are the strangest moves and hearing them helps.
- **Chapter 5, stage table.** Read stage number, name and the "by the end" line for each of the ten. Skip the durations.
- **Chapter 6, flash ladder (9 rows) and the Japanese grades (6 rows).** Read the nine levels with their example rounds, slowly, as rounds: "Level one. Three, five, one. Nine." Summarise the Japanese grades in a sentence: "from three one-digit numbers in five seconds at the bottom, to fifteen three-digit numbers in three seconds at tenth dan."
- **Chapter 7, Bead Score rungs (20 rows).** Read rungs one to nine in full, then say "rungs ten to twenty climb through three-digit numbers and flash rounds to the world record; the full ladder is in the book and in the trainer." Skip the Times ladder; say it exists and has ten rungs.
- **Formula card.** Not recorded.

## Pronunciation and small things

- *soroban*: SOH-roh-bahn. *anzan*: AHN-zahn. *kyu*: kyoo. *dan*: dahn. *Miyamoto*: mee-yah-MOH-toh.
- "ACT" is said as letters. "D1" is "Division One."
- Numbers in worked examples are read as digits on the rod, not as sums: "the rod shows four, you want to add one."
- The em-dash signature at the end of chapter 9 is read as a beat, then "Hunter."
- No music under the voice. A short sting between chapters is fine.
