## Where this stage goes

**The level goal.** Ten 3-digit lines in the head, 3-digit flash, and mental 2 × 2 multiplication. This is expert-level mental arithmetic.

**In plain words.** Ten three-digit numbers added in the head. Three-digit flash. Two-digit times two-digit mentally. Expert-level mental math. The one to film.

**How long.** 3–4 months at fifteen minutes a day, six days a week.

Nothing new to learn here. Everything gets bigger and faster. The wooden soroban becomes a warm-up tool, and every drill is mental.

Expect plateaus. They last about two weeks and then break overnight, usually after a day when nothing seemed to happen. Keep the sessions short and daily, and keep the drill the same size through the flat weeks.

Ten mixed 3-digit lines in the head is the format of the Japanese 1st kyu mental exam, the top grade below dan. Your child will meet it in the second lesson.

### The drills in this stage

- **Mental.** Five, then ten 3-digit lines in the head, plus and minus. Times and divide on the mental beads.
- **Flash.** Five, then ten 3-digit numbers, at 1 second and then 0.8 seconds.
- **Life drills.** Money sums from real receipts, and sums that drop below zero.

### Accuracy before speed

Pass marks drop a little here, to 75 or 80 percent, because the problems are long. The rule does not change. Speed up only after three good sheets in a row. On the ten-line page, under two minutes a problem is the first goal. Exam speed is under forty seconds, and that comes later.

### Where this sits on the abacus ladders

Prodigy is the Advanced level in our map to UCMAS. Ten 3-digit lines in the head is the Japanese 1st kyu mental format, and Bead Score rung 15. Five 3-digit numbers flashed at one second is rung 14, around 2nd dan on the flash anzan ladder.

> ✎ Plateaus last two weeks and break overnight. Keep the daily drill at the same size.

### The move

Decimals change nothing on the beads. Pick a rod to be the ones, one with a dot on the beam. The rods to its right are tenths and hundredths.

So $12.75 + $3.50 is 1275 + 350 on the beads, with the dot two rods from the right. The beads show 1625. Read it as $16.25.

Then take it shopping. Add the receipt in your head before the till does.

## Lesson 8.9 · Below zero

*Lesson 65 of 76. Stage 8, lesson 9 of 9. About 10 minutes. You need no soroban. A pencil for the answers only.*

**By the end, your child can** work a sum that drops below zero and say the answer with its minus sign.

### The move

A soroban cannot show a number below zero. So soroban users borrow from an empty rod on the far left.

Take 20 − 35. It will not fit. Borrow 100: now it is 120 − 35 = 85. The beads show 85. But you borrowed 100, so give it back. 100 − 85 = 15, and the answer is below zero: minus 15.

Try a few on paper first, then in the head. This is rare in exams and useful in life, from temperatures to bank balances.

**Worked example.** 20 − 35, with a borrowed 100.

- 20 − 35 will not fit. Borrow 100 from an empty rod on the left. Now it is 120 − 35.
- Work it on the beads: [beads: 85].
- You borrowed 100, so give it back: 100 − 85 = 15, and it is below zero.
- Read it: **−15**.

> ✎ Use a thermometer or a game score to show why numbers below zero are real.

### Drill

No soroban. Borrow 100 from an empty rod when a line will not fit, then give it back at the end. Write the answer with its minus sign. **Pass mark:** 8 of 10 right. Not yet? Same page tomorrow.
