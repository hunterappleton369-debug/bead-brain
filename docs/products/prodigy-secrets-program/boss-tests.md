# Boss tests: all ten stages

One printed test for the end of each stage. The parts, the number of problems, the pass marks and the time limits are the same as the trainer's boss tests.

## How a boss works

A boss is a short timed test in two to four parts. Each part has a pass mark and a time limit. Pass every part and the stage is done. Miss one part and your child retakes that part another day, not the whole boss.

No hints during a boss. No "free beads, or friend?" from the chair. Set a timer for each part, hand over the page, and sit on your hands. When the timer goes, pencils down.

Most children need two or three tries at a boss. That is the design. Each part matches one or two lessons, listed on the last page of every stage file, so a failed part tells you exactly which drill to repeat this week.

Saturday is boss day in the weekly plan. Never on a tired day. The score is meant to show what your child can do, not how late it is.

For parts marked "on your soroban, a grown-up checks each one", your child works on the real soroban and you check each rod before the next problem. In the trainer those parts run on the screen abacus. Flash parts at 0.7 seconds or faster need the trainer for a true beat. On paper, read them at your fastest clear pace and treat the result as practice.
