## Where this stage goes

**The level goal.** Fifteen 3-digit numbers flashed at 0.3 seconds or faster, and 3 × 3 multiplication in the head. Competition level.

**In plain words.** Fifteen numbers flashed at 0.3 seconds. Three-digit times three-digit in the head. Competition speed. There is no top to this ladder.

**How long.** open-ended at fifteen minutes a day, six days a week.

There is no boss your child must beat to finish. Legend is a set of targets to chase for as long as it is fun. Each one cleared goes on the chart.

The world record for fifteen 3-digit numbers is 1.61 seconds in total, about a tenth of a second a number. Nobody needs to get there. But the ladder has no top, and that is the fun of it.

Keep the routine the same as it has been since Sprout. Fifteen minutes. Same chair. Same time. A child at this level still gets better the same way a child at Stage 1 does.

### The drills in this stage

- **Flash.** Fifteen 3-digit numbers at 0.4, then 0.3, then 0.2 seconds. Trainer only for the true speed.
- **Mental.** 3-digit times 3-digit, and fifteen 3-digit lines with plus and minus, both in the head.

### Accuracy before speed

Pass marks sit at 75 percent here, and the long head sums at two in three. Even at this level the rule holds. Hold a speed until it is calm. A Legend who guesses at 0.2 seconds is not a Legend at 0.3.

### Where this sits on the abacus ladders

Legend is competition level, past the end of every program in our map. On the flash anzan ladder, fifteen 3-digit numbers at 0.4 seconds is about 8th dan. At 0.3 seconds it is about 9th dan. At 0.2 seconds it is 10th dan, Bead Score rung 20, the top of the ladder. On the Times ladder, 3-digit times 3-digit is rung 10.

## Lesson 10.4 · Mental: 3-digit × 3-digit

*Lesson 75 of 76. Stage 10, lesson 4 of 5. About 15 minutes. You need no soroban. A pencil for the answers only.*

**By the end, your child can** times a 3-digit number by a 3-digit number in the head.

### The move

Nine small answers. Keep the rhythm, and keep the landing rods exact.

Each digit of the top number works through all three digits of the bottom number, one rod right each step. Then the next digit starts one rod further right than the last one did.

**Worked example.** 386 × 472 on the mental beads.

- 3 × 4 = 12. It lands on the hundred thousands and ten thousands rods. Beads: [beads: 120000].
- 3 × 7 = 21. It lands on the ten thousands and thousands rods. Beads: [beads: 141000].
- 3 × 2 = 06. It lands on the thousands and hundreds rods. Beads: [beads: 141600].
- 8 × 4 = 32. It lands on the ten thousands and thousands rods. Beads: [beads: 173600].
- 8 × 7 = 56. It lands on the thousands and hundreds rods. Beads: [beads: 179200].
- 8 × 2 = 16. It lands on the hundreds and tens rods. Beads: [beads: 179360].
- 6 × 4 = 24. It lands on the thousands and hundreds rods. Beads: [beads: 181760].
- 6 × 7 = 42. It lands on the hundreds and tens rods. Beads: [beads: 182180].
- 6 × 2 = 12. It lands on the tens and ones rods. Beads: [beads: 182192].
- Read it: **182,192**.
