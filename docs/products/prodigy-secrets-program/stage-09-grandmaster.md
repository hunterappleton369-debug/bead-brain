## Where this stage goes

**The level goal.** Ten 3-digit numbers flashed at half a second, four-digit mental sums, 3 × 2 and 4 ÷ 2 in the head.

**In plain words.** Ten three-digit numbers flashed at half a second each. Four-digit sums in the head. Dan-level targets, the grades Japanese club kids reach after years.

**How long.** 4–6 months at fifteen minutes a day, six days a week.

From here the course gives drills and targets, not new lessons. The technique is finished. What grows is the size and speed of the picture.

These targets sit around the Japanese dan grades, which club children reach after years of classes. Short daily sessions beat long weekly ones on the way there. There is no promise on the date. Every child is on their own clock here.

### The drills in this stage

- **Flash.** Ten 3-digit numbers at 0.7 seconds, then 0.5 seconds. These speeds need the trainer for the true beat.
- **Mental.** 4-digit lines, 3-digit times 2-digit, 4-digit divided by 2-digit, all in the head.
- **Listening.** Ten 3-digit numbers read aloud at about one a second, exam style.

### Accuracy before speed

The rule matters more here than anywhere. A child who passes 0.7 seconds at 8 of 10 is ready for 0.5. A child at 5 of 10 is guessing, and guessing at speed builds nothing. Hold each speed until it is calm. Then move.

### Where this sits on the abacus ladders

Grandmaster is the dan level in our map to UCMAS, the Grand end of the Advanced module. On the flash anzan ladder, ten 3-digit numbers at 0.7 seconds is about 5th dan, Bead Score rung 16. At 0.5 seconds it is around 7th dan, rung 17. On the Times ladder, this stage covers rungs 8 and 9.

> ✎ Short and daily beats long and weekly, more than ever.

## Lesson 9.3 · Mental: 4-digit lines

*Lesson 68 of 76. Stage 9, lesson 3 of 6. About 12 minutes. You need no soroban. A pencil for the answers only.*

**By the end, your child can** add and take away five 4-digit lines in the head.

### The move

Four rods in the picture. Research suggests this is near the limit of visual working memory, so it may take months to feel steady.

Five lines, mixed. If four rods will not hold, go back to 3-digit head sums for a week, then try again.

**Worked example.** The first two lines of a 4-digit head sum.

- Start with [beads: 5786].
- **+ 3945.** Thousands rod, plus 3: the beads are free. Hundreds rod, plus 9: take away 1, carry 1 to the left. Thousands rod, the carry (plus 1): the beads are free. Tens rod, plus 4: take away 6, carry 1 to the left. Hundreds rod, the carry (plus 1): the beads are free. Ones rod, plus 5: take away 5, carry 1 to the left. Tens rod, the carry (plus 1): the beads are free. Now [beads: 9731].
- Read it: **9,731**.

> ✎ This page can take months, so judge it by the month, not by the day.

## Lesson 9.5 · Mental: 4-digit ÷ 2-digit

*Lesson 70 of 76. Stage 9, lesson 5 of 6. About 12 minutes. You need no soroban. A pencil for the answers only.*

**By the end, your child can** divide a 4-digit number by a 2-digit number in the head.

### The move

Guess, times, take away. Twice. The guess comes from the first digits, as on the soroban.

The hard part is the times step, a 2-digit number times a guess, held while the take-away happens. Go slowly. The beads wait.

**Worked example.** 2,752 ÷ 32 on the mental beads.

- Set 2,752 on the beads: [beads: 2752].
- 32 does not go into 2. Use the next digit too.
- 32 does not go into 27. Use the next digit too.
- 32 into 275: 8. Take away 2,560. Beads: [beads: 192]. Answer so far: 8.
- 32 into 192: 6. Take away 192. Beads: [beads: 0]. Answer so far: 86.
- Read it: **86**.

> ✎ If your child needs to say the guess out loud, let them, because it frees up the picture.

### Drill

No soroban. Guess, multiply, take away, on the picture. Write only the answer. **Pass mark:** 8 of 10 right. Not yet? Same page tomorrow.
