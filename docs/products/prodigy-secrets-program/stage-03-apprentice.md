Nine lessons on carrying and borrowing. Big friends make 10, and they are the moment the soroban gets faster than fingers and paper.

## Where this stage goes

**The level goal.** Carry and borrow between columns with the eighteen big-friend (10) formulas, in 5-line problems.

**In plain words.** Carry and borrow between columns with the "make 10" trick. Five-line sums on the beads. The soroban is now faster than fingers and paper.

**How long.** 5–6 weeks at fifteen minutes a day, six days a week.

A rod holds 9 at most. When a sum goes past 9, one bead moves to the rod on the left, and the rod gives back the difference from 10. When a take-away needs more than the rod has, one bead comes back from the left.

Two numbers that make 10 are big friends: 1 and 9, 2 and 8, 3 and 7, 4 and 6, 5 and 5. To add a number that will not fit, take away its big friend and carry 1. To take away a number that is not there, add its big friend, then borrow 1.

Order matters on a real soroban. Work the rod you are on first, then the rod to its left. For adding: take away the friend, then carry. For taking away: add the friend, then borrow. One rule for both.

### The drills in this stage

- **Two rods.** A start number up to 99 and one digit. Watch the tens rod change on every carry and borrow.
- **Written.** Five-line columns of 2-digit numbers, adds first, then plus and minus mixed.
- **Dictation.** Read a 5-line column out loud while your child works it on the beads.
- **Mental.** Three, then four one-digit numbers in the head, now crossing ten.

### Accuracy before speed

Carries feel backwards for about a week. That is normal. Hold the pass marks at 9 of 10 on the two-rod pages and do not add a clock anywhere in this stage. A child who carries correctly and slowly will be fast by Stage 5. A child who carries quickly and wrong has to unlearn it.

### Where this sits on the abacus ladders

Five-line columns of 2-digit numbers are the format of 10th kyu, the first graded level of the Japanese soroban exams. In UCMAS terms this is still Foundation, the last part of it. The mental lesson at the end of the stage is Bead Score rung 2 and 3 work: any two digits, then a running total, crossing ten.

## Lesson 3.1 · +9, +8, +7

*Lesson 17 of 76. Stage 3, lesson 1 of 9. About 15 minutes. You need your soroban and a grown-up beside you.*

**By the end, your child can** add 9, 8 or 7 to a rod when it will not fit, by taking away the big friend and carrying 1.

### The move

Set 4. Add 9. There is no room: 4 and 9 is more than one rod can hold.

So use the big friend of 9, which is 1. Take 1 away from this rod. Then carry 1 to the tens rod. The rod shows 3 and the tens rod shows 1. That is 13.

Plus 8 takes away 2 and carries. Plus 7 takes away 3 and carries. Say it every time: "plus 8, take away 2, carry 1."

**Worked example.** Start at 4. Add 9, then 8.

- Start with [beads: 4].
- **+ 9.** Ones rod, plus 9: take away 1, carry 1 to the left. Tens rod, the carry (plus 1): the beads are free. Now [beads: 13].
- **+ 8.** Ones rod, plus 8: take away 2, carry 1 to the left. Tens rod, the carry (plus 1): the beads are free. Now [beads: 21].
- Read it: **21**.

> ✎ Ask your child to say the order out loud, friend off first and then the carry, until the hand does it without the words.

## Lesson 3.4 · Carry chains

*Lesson 20 of 76. Stage 3, lesson 4 of 9. About 15 minutes. You need your soroban and a grown-up beside you.*

**By the end, your child can** carry across two or three rods in a row, in 5-line columns of 2-digit adds.

### The move

Sometimes the carry itself does not fit. 99 + 1: the ones rod takes away 9 and carries. The tens rod already shows 9, so it takes away 9 and carries again. The hundreds rod gets 1. That is 100.

Each carry is just another plus 1, with the same rules. Your child already knows them.

Now real sums: five lines of two digits, adding only, with small and big friends mixed. Use the soroban.

**Worked example.** Start at 97. Add 4, then 99.

## Lesson 3.5 · −9, −8, −7: borrowing

*Lesson 21 of 76. Stage 3, lesson 5 of 9. About 15 minutes. You need your soroban and a grown-up beside you.*

**By the end, your child can** take away 9, 8 or 7 when the rod does not have enough, by adding the big friend and borrowing 1.

### The move

Set 13. Take away 9. The ones rod shows 3. There is no 9 to take.

So add the big friend of 9, which is 1. The ones rod shows 4. Then borrow: take 1 from the tens rod. That is minus 10. Add 1 and take away 10, and you have taken away 9. That is 13 − 9 = 4.

Minus 8 adds 2, then borrows. Minus 7 adds 3, then borrows. Say it as you move: "Minus 9: add 1, borrow 1 from the left."

## Lesson 3.7 · Borrow chains and mixed problems

*Lesson 23 of 76. Stage 3, lesson 7 of 9. About 15 minutes. You need your soroban and a grown-up beside you.*

**By the end, your child can** borrow across empty rods and work 5-line columns with plus and minus mixed.

### The move

100 − 1: the ones rod is empty, so add 9, then borrow 1 from the tens. The tens rod is empty too, so it does the same: add 9, then borrow 1 from the hundreds. 100 − 1 = 99.

A borrow chain is a carry chain run backwards. Each borrow is a minus 1 on the next rod, with the same rules.

Now plus and minus mixed, five lines. Every move from the first three stages is in play. This is the real thing.

**Worked example.** Start at 100. Take away 1, then add 45.

## The Apprentice Boss

Take the boss when every lesson is passed and a 5-line column feels calm. Part 1 maps to Lessons 3.1 to 3.6. Part 2 maps to 3.4 and 3.7. Part 3 maps to 3.9.

The printed test is **Boss 3** in boss-tests.md. It has 3 parts:

- **Carry or borrow.** 10 moves on your soroban. Need 9 of 10 in 2 minutes 30 seconds.
- **2-digit, 5 lines.** 6 problems on your soroban. Need 5 of 6 in 5 minutes.
- **Mental.** 5 problems, four 1-digit numbers each. Need 4 of 5 in 2 minutes 30 seconds.

## What the trainer adds to this stage

The trainer's two-rod drill draws the tens rod right beside the ones rod, so your child sees the carry land. That small picture does a lot of teaching in the first week of this stage.

It also mixes carries and borrows at random once both are learned, which is hard to do by hand without a problem book. And it shows the move after any miss, so a wrong carry gets fixed on the spot instead of at the end of the page.
