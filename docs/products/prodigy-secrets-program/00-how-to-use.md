# How to use the Prodigy Secrets Program

Two pages to read once, before Lesson 1.1. Everything after this is one lesson at a time.

This book is the whole course on paper. It has all 10 stages, from the first bead to fifteen numbers flashed in the head. Every lesson in the trainer has a page here, in the same order, with the same pass marks. It is yours to keep.

You do not need to be a math person to run it. Your job is a chair, one question and patience.

## The fifteen minutes

Same spot, same time, six days a week. The kitchen table after breakfast works. Four parts, always in this order.

1. **Warm-up, 2 minutes.** Read ten numbers off the beads, or do the first quest card of the stage.
2. **Lesson or drill, 8 to 10 minutes.** One lesson a day at most. Read "The move" together, look at the worked example, then do the drill.
3. **Mental, 5 minutes.** From Stage 2 on, push the soroban away and do a few sums in the head, or a flash round. This is the part that builds the picture. Never skip it.
4. **Log it, 1 minute.** Say the day's score or time out loud. Write it at the top of the page.

Short and daily beats long and weekly. A few days off and the picture goes dull, so protect the streak.

## The week

- **Monday to Thursday:** a lesson.
- **Friday:** the quest cards only. They sit after the last lesson of each stage.
- **Saturday:** the boss, if every lesson in the stage is passed. Otherwise a quest card.
- **Sunday:** off, or a few numbers in the car.

Every two to four weeks from Stage 5 on, swap the mental part for the Bead Score test in the trainer.

## The hand

Two fingers touch the beads. The thumb pushes the bottom beads up to the beam. The index finger pushes them down, and moves the top bead both ways. Some books call the top bead the heaven bead and the bottom ones earth beads. It is the same soroban.

Work left to right: hundreds, then tens, then ones. It is the opposite of school, and it is the reason math in the head becomes possible. Your child adds a number in the same order they say it.

Say the move out loud. "Plus 7: take away 3, carry 1." The words fall away on their own once the hand knows the move.

## The four move colors

Every bead move in addition and take-away is one of four kinds. This book and the trainer use the same colors.

- **Green: direct.** The beads you need are free. Move them.
- **Orange: small friend.** Make 5. Add 5 and take away the friend, or the other way round. Stage 2, eight moves.
- **Blue: big friend.** Make 10. Take away the friend and carry 1, or add the friend and borrow 1. Stage 3, eighteen moves.
- **Purple: combination.** Both friends at once. Stage 4, eight moves.

That makes 34 moves. After Stage 4 there are no new bead rules, only speed and the picture in the head.

## How a lesson page runs

Every lesson page has the same parts in the same order, so nobody has to think about what comes next.

- **By the end, your child can.** The goal in one line. Read it out loud.
- **The move.** A short read for the two of you. Then the worked example, step by step. A picture like [beads: 694] shows the soroban at that step.
- **The parent line.** One sentence for whoever is in the chair. It is the only coaching you need that day.
- **The drill.** Ten problems, built so they need only the moves taught so far. Every one was checked on a soroban model before it was printed. The pass mark is printed above the drill. Hit it and the lesson is done. Miss it and do the same page tomorrow. Nothing is lost.
- **Stuck?** What to say when your child freezes. Never the answer.
- **Next.** The next lesson, or the boss.

Answers sit at the back of each stage file, so the page in front of your child never shows them.

> ✎ When your child hesitates, ask "free beads, or friend?" and wait. Do not touch the beads.

## Your one question

When your child stalls on a rod, ask "free beads, or friend?" Then wait, longer than feels right. The decision is the skill. Every time you make it for them, you take that rep away.

When they get it right, praise the move they said, not the answer. "I heard you say take away 3, carry 1. That was it."

## Accuracy before speed

Nine out of ten slowly beats seven out of ten fast, every time. Speed up only after three good sheets in a row. The clock looks after itself.

## When to take a boss

Each stage ends with a boss test in boss-tests.md. Take it when every lesson in the stage is passed, on a Saturday, never on a tired day. Every part is timed, with no hints. Pass every part and the next stage opens.

Most children need two or three tries. That is the design. Each part matches a lesson, so a missed part tells you exactly which page to repeat that week.
