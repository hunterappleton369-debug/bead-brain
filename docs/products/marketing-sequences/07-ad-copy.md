# Facebook ad set: five angles

The five test angles from `docs/FUNNEL.md`. Each has three primary texts, three headlines and one video script of 30 seconds or less. Every ad sends parents to the kit page.

## How to run it

- **Link:** {{link_kit}} (the free kit page).
- **Test plan:** one ad set per angle, same audience, same budget. Hold the winner, then test new primary texts inside it.
- **Audience:** parents 32 to 48, top 20% household income, interests in private schools, Montessori, Kumon, Mathnasium, Khan Academy Kids and chess for kids. Add a lookalike of kit buyers once there are 500.
- **Hook, story, offer:** each primary text opens with a hook, gives a short story, then makes the offer. If an angle loses, check which of the three is weak before you kill it.

## Rules every ad must follow

- **No health claims.** Never say the abacus helps, treats or fixes ADHD, focus or attention. Hunter may say he had ADHD. Never say or hint that the viewer's child has it.
- **No IQ or memory claims.** We can say what the studies found about arithmetic. Nothing more.
- **No before and after.** No split images. No grade reports. No "from this to this" visuals.
- **No income claims.** The ads sell the kit, not instructor work.
- **No counts we can't prove.** Don't say "thousands of kids."
- **Footage we own.** Film Hunter and your own families. Never cut in the NBC, PBS or TikTok clips. Those belong to someone else.
