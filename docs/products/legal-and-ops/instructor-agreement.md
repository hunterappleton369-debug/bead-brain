## 3. Your certification

1. You are certified when you pass the exam. The certification is yours for life. We cannot take it back, and it never needs renewing.
2. Two things sit on top of the certification and are separate from it: your licence to use the Child Phenoms name and badge (section 5), and your directory listing (section 10). 3. We may ask certified instructors to take a short refresher when the method changes. We will give at least 60 days' notice and charge nothing for it.

## 4. You run your own business

1. You are an independent business. You are not our employee, agent, partner, joint venturer or franchisee.
2. You choose whether to teach, when, where, how many students, and what to charge.
3. You find your own students. We do not assign students, territories or quotas, and you can teach other subjects or programs too.
4. You pay your own taxes, insurance, licences, rent, supplies and costs. We do not pay you and we do not take a share of what you earn.
5. You cannot sign contracts or make promises in our name.

## 5. Using our name and badge

We give you a limited, personal, non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable licence to use the words "Certified Phenom Instructor" and the badge, only to say truthfully that you are certified by Child Phenoms.

You may:

- put the badge on your flyers, website, social profiles and class materials
- say "Certified Phenom Instructor" or "Certified by Child Phenoms"
- link to your directory listing and to childphenoms.com

You may not:

- use the Child Phenoms logo or name as your business name, domain name or social handle
- change the badge's colours, shape or wording
- say or suggest that you work for Child Phenoms or that we run, supervise or guarantee your classes
- use the name or badge after it ends

## 6. No earnings claims

1. We make no promise that you will earn any money. What you earn depends on your effort, your area and your rates, and many instructors teach for free.
2. When you advertise your classes, do not promise parents results we cannot back up: no grade, test score, IQ, memory, attention, ADHD or "genius" claims, and no "results in weeks".
3. You may say what the program teaches, how classes run and what you charge.
4. Do not quote income figures to other people as a reason to become an instructor.

## 7. Child safety

Teaching children is a position of trust. These expectations apply to every class, in person or online.

1. **Parents stay in the loop.** Talk to parents, not children, about schedules, payments and progress. Do not message a child directly by text, social media or private chat.
2. **Open doors.** In person, teach where another adult can see or hear: a parent nearby, an open door, a public room, or two adults present.
3. **Online classes.** A parent must know when the class runs and be home. Use a meeting link sent to the parent. Do not record a session without the parent's written permission.
4. **Photos and videos.** Do not photograph or film a child, or post any image of one, without the parent's written permission.
5. **Touch.** Only the light touch needed to guide a hand on the abacus, and only if the child is comfortable.
6. **Discipline.** Never physical, never humiliating. Speak to the parent.
7. **Report concerns.** Follow your state's child-abuse reporting law. If you see or suspect abuse, call the local child protective services or 911. Tell us too.
8. **Local rules.** Follow the laws where you teach, including any licence needed to run classes for children.

## 8. Students' information

1. When you collect parents' and children's details for your classes, you are responsible for them. Collect only what you need, keep it secure and delete it when a family leaves.
2. Student profiles in your instructor account are created by you or by the parent, with the parent's permission. Use a first name or nickname only.
3. Do not add families to any mailing list, including ours, without their consent.
4. Our Privacy Policy covers what Child Phenoms itself does with data in the trainer.

## 9. Our materials

1. You may teach with the course, the Launch Kit and the handouts, and print handouts for students enrolled in your classes.
2. You may not sell, share, post or record our lessons, videos, software or written materials, or make a competing course from them.
3. Families in your classes who want the trainer at home buy their own membership, or you add them to your instructor account.

## 10. Your directory listing

1. Your listing shows what you give us: name, city or area, whether you teach online, how to contact you, and your rates if you choose.
2. Keep it accurate. Tell us within 14 days if anything changes.
3. We can edit or remove a listing that is wrong, out of date or breaks this agreement.

## 11. Ending the licence and the listing

1. **You** can stop at any time by emailing hello@childphenoms.com. Refunds follow the 30-day money-back guarantee.
2. **We** can end your licence to use the name and badge, and take down your listing, if you break this agreement. We will email you, explain why, and give you 14 days to fix it, unless the problem is about a child's safety, a crime, or a false claim, in which case it ends at once.
3. When it ends, you stop using the badge and the title "Certified Phenom Instructor" in your marketing within 7 days and remove them from your materials and profiles. Your directory listing comes down. You keep your certificate and the record that you passed, and you may still say you completed the Child Phenoms certification course.
4. Sections 6, 9.2, 12 and 13 continue after it ends.

## 12. Responsibility

1. You are responsible for your own classes, premises, students' safety and conduct, and for the promises you make to parents.
2. You agree to cover our costs if someone makes a claim against us because of your classes or because you broke this agreement.
3. We recommend general liability insurance that covers teaching children.
4. Our total liability to you under this agreement is limited to the $297 you paid.
