## 10. My child is stuck (or very young)

**Subject:** Getting unstuck

Hi {first},

Thanks for telling me. Every child hits a wall. It usually means one move isn't solid yet.

Try this for three days:

1. Go back one lesson to the last one that felt easy.
2. Do 5 problems slowly. Say the move out loud: "free beads, or friend?"
3. Then 5 more from the lesson that was hard.

Praise the move, not the answer. Speed comes later.

{If the child is under 6:} Younger children often take twice as long on the first two stages. Ten-minute sessions are plenty.

Send me a short video of a session if you like, and I'll tell you what I see. Tell me {child}'s age and which lesson too.

Hunter
