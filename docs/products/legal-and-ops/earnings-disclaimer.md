# Earnings disclaimer

What we can and cannot say about money you might make as a Certified Phenom Instructor.

Effective date: [HUNTER: go-live date]

## 1. No income promise

Child Phenoms makes no promise or guarantee that you will earn any money as an instructor. Buying the certification is a purchase of training and tools. It is not a job offer, a franchise or a business opportunity.

## 2. The one number we print

Our certification page says: "Most instructors land between $20 and $40 a session. Your rates are yours to set."

That range is an example of what tutoring sessions often cost. It is not a record of what our instructors have earned, and it is not a forecast of what you will earn.

## 3. What actually decides your income

What you earn, if anything, depends on things we do not control:

- how much time and effort you put in
- where you live, and what families there will pay
- the rates you choose
- how many families you reach
- your teaching skill, and how your students do

Many instructors teach their own children or teach for free. Some will earn nothing.

## 4. Costs you may have

Running classes can cost money: space, supplies, abacuses for students, advertising, insurance, a background check and local licences. These are your costs, not ours.

## 5. You are your own business

Certified instructors are independent. They are not our employees, agents or partners. You set your own prices, find your own students and pay your own taxes. The Instructor Agreement has the details.

## 6. Stories and examples

Any story, example or figure about teaching or income on our pages, in emails, in the course or in the Launch Kit is an illustration. It is not typical, and you should not rely on it when deciding whether to buy.
