# Handout template

The one-page layout every Phenom-U practice sheet uses, zone by zone. A blank copy to print sits at the end.

## The page, top to bottom

1. **Top bar.** The Child Phenoms name on the left. On the right, a chip that says what kind of drill this is: written, mental, flash, listening, bead reading, setting or times tables. "Timed" is added when the sheet has a time goal.
2. **Title.** The stage, the lesson number and the lesson name, the same words the app uses. The stage color runs through the chips and the pass box.
3. **Header box.** Name, date, time and score, with the pass mark on the right, such as "Pass: 17 of 20".
4. **Three short lines.** "Today's move" says the one idea of the lesson. "How" says how to work the sheet. "Stuck?" gives the hint to read out loud when your child gets stuck.
5. **Formula chips.** On lessons that teach a new friend, the moves are printed as chips, such as "+1 = +5 −4". Small friends are orange, big friends are blue, and moves that need both are purple.
6. **The problems.** Twenty problems in two rows of ten, set the way soroban practice sheets set them: numbers stacked in a column, the sign on the left, a rule under the last line and a big box for the answer. Times tables, multiplication and division sit in four columns of five, each with a wide answer box. Bead-reading sheets show a soroban picture in each cell.
7. **Footer.** "Work left to right. Say the move." on the left. The parent line for the stage on the right.
8. **Answer strip.** At the very bottom, under a dashed line. Fold it under before your child starts. Unfold it to mark the sheet.

Flash and listening sheets add **page 2** for the grown-up. It holds the numbers to read out loud, the beat to read them at, and the answer for each round.

Boss-prep sheets use the same zones. The problems are split into parts, one per part of the boss test, and each part shows its own pass mark and time limit.

## Page size

Letter or A4, half-inch margins, one sheet per page. Big boxes, so a seven-year-old's numbers fit.
