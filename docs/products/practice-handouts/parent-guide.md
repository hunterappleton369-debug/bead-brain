# Practice handouts: the parent guide

One page on which sheet to print, how to time it, and when your child is ready to move on.

## Which sheet, which day

Every lesson has its own sheet. The file name tells you which: `stage-03-carry-chain.md` is Stage 3, the Carry chains lesson. The [handout catalog](handout-catalog.md) lists them all in order.

- **Lesson days (Monday to Thursday).** Do the lesson in the app first. Then print that lesson's sheet. Do it the same day or the next morning.
- **Quest day (Friday).** No new lesson. Pick the sheet your child found hardest this week and do it again with fresh numbers.
- **Boss day (Saturday).** Do the boss-prep sheet the day before the boss. It has the same parts, pass marks and time limits as the real test.
- **Sunday.** Off.

A sheet takes 8 to 12 minutes. That fits inside the 15-minute routine from the Playbook.

## What each kind of sheet asks for

- **Written.** Your child works each problem on the soroban and writes what the beads show.
- **Mental.** The soroban goes away. Your child sees the beads in their head and writes only the answer.
- **Flash and listening.** Page 1 has the answer boxes. Page 2 is yours. You read the numbers out loud at the beat it gives. Do not let your child see page 2.
- **Bead reading and setting.** Early sheets for Stage 1. Your child reads beads off a picture, or sets a number and you check it.

## How to time it

1. Fold the answer strip under before you start, so it hides behind the page.
2. Start a stopwatch when the pencil first touches the page.
3. Stop it when the last box is filled. Write the time in the Time box.
4. Unfold the strip and mark the sheet together. Write the score.

Right answers come first. Only start to chase a faster time once your child has hit the pass mark. Every proven abacus program works this way: first right, then fast.

## What the score means

Each sheet prints its pass mark at the top, such as "Pass: 17 of 20". It is the same target the lesson uses in the app.

- **At or above the pass mark.** Move on to the next lesson.
- **A little under.** Print a fresh copy of the same sheet tomorrow. Same lesson, new numbers.
- **Under half, two days in a row.** Go back one lesson for a day. Then come back.
- **A time goal on the sheet.** Some sheets say "in 10 min". The score counts only if it is done in that time.

## When it goes wrong

Each sheet has a "Stuck?" line. Read it out loud and let your child try again on the beads. Do not give the answer. Ask "free beads, or friend?" and wait. Playbook chapter 8 has more lines for hard days.
