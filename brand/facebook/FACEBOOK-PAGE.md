## Set it up, step by step

1. Go to https://business.facebook.com. If you have no Child Phenoms business portfolio yet, click **Create a business portfolio** and name it Child Phenoms.
2. Go to https://facebook.com/pages/create.
3. **Page name:** Child Phenoms. **Category:** type Education and pick it. **Bio:** paste bio 1. Click **Create Page**.
4. Click **Next** on the contact step for now, or add the website.
5. **Add profile picture:** upload `profile-picture.png`. **Add cover photo:** upload `cover-photo.png`. Drag it so the headline sits in the middle. Click **Save**.
6. On the Page, click **Edit** next to the details. Add the website, the username @childphenoms and Tutor/Teacher as a second category. Choose "No hours available".
7. Click **+ Add action button**. Pick **Sign up**. Paste the link from "Action button" above. Click **Save**.
8. Make the first post from the text above. Click the **…** on the post and choose **Pin to top of Page**.
9. In Business settings, go to **Accounts → Pages** and add the Page to the portfolio if it isn't there.

**Done when** the Page shows the bolt, the cover, the bio and a Sign up button, and the pinned post is at the top.
