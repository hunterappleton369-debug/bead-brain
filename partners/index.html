How it works

  1. Apply. Show us a couple of recent videos and tell us who watches you. About 1,000 followers is plenty. So are a few videos over 10,000 views.

Questions

Do I need a big following?

No. About 1,000 followers, or a few videos over 10,000 views. Who watches you matters more than how many.

Do I get a free kit?

Everyone pays the $6.95 shipping, our own team included, so every partner has the same experience a parent has. Your first referred kit pays you $10, which covers it.

How do you know a parent came from me?

Your link (childphenoms.com/go/YOURCODE) remembers you for 30 days on that parent's phone or computer, so a parent who taps it today and orders next week still counts for you. If a parent taps two partners' links, the most recent one counts. There is nothing to type at checkout.

When do I get paid?

Once a month by PayPal, around the 15th, for kits that are 30 days old and were not refunded. A kit ordered November 10 clears December 10 and is paid in the December run. No minimum.

What if a parent returns the kit?

Refunded within 30 days, it does not count. That is the only reason for the 30-day wait.

Where can I post?

Anywhere parents watch you: TikTok, Instagram Reels and Stories, YouTube Shorts, Facebook groups and pages, Pinterest, a newsletter. The link works everywhere. Parents order in one place, childphenoms.com.

What can I say about results?

The hub has a plain list. Short version: talk about what abacus training is known for (focus, memory, confidence, speed with numbers), show the record-setting kids as what is possible, and do not promise a specific result by a specific date.

Is this an MLM? Do I pay anything?

No and no. You never pay us or buy anything. You earn when a parent orders a kit. No downline, no levels, no recruiting.

Can my kids be in the videos?

Yes, and kids on the beads are the best footage there is. You do the talking about the offer and the link.
